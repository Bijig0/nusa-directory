Batam is a busy transit city, especially on weekends when visitors from Singapore cross by ferry. The three areas that appear most often in ads are **Nagoya**, **Harbour Bay** and **Batam Centre**.

## Nagoya
The classic nightlife hub. Two- to four-star hotels line the streets around Nagoya Hill. Many outcall massage providers are based here for quick hotel access.

## Harbour Bay
Next to the international ferry terminal. Ideal for short visits; most hotels accept guests at any hour.

## Batam Centre
Offices and malls, quieter. A good choice if you prefer a more private setting.

## Safety tips
1. Always chat on WhatsApp first and ask for a recent photo.
2. Never transfer money in advance.
3. Report suspicious ads with the "Report" button on the ad page.
