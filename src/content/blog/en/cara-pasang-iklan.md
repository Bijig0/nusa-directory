## Title
The title is read in listings and on Google. Include a nickname, age, one highlight and the area: *"Sari 23, friendly & patient, hotel visits in Nagoya"*. Avoid all-caps and symbol spam.

## Photos
The first photo is the cover shown in listings. Use bright photos with a clear face or silhouette and suggestive clothing without explicit nudity (see the Content Policy). We strip location data (GPS) automatically and add a watermark so photos are not stolen.

## Rates
Fill in the 1-hour rate so the ad appears when visitors filter by price. The 2-hour and overnight rates are optional.

## Renewals & promotions
Ads run for 30 days and can be renewed for free from the dashboard. In busy cities, **Bump** returns the ad to the top, **Featured** adds a gold frame for 7 days, and **VIP** places the ad in the homepage carousel for 30 days.
