---
title: "7 safety tips before meeting a companion from an online ad"
description: "How to verify an ad, avoid upfront-transfer scams, and protect your privacy."
published: 2026-08-20
tags: ["safety"]
---
1. **Unlock the number on the site, then chat first.** Ask for a fresh photo in a specific pose to confirm it is the same person.
2. **Never pay in advance.** Ads asking for a deposit, an "admin fee" or phone credit are almost always scams. Report them.
3. **Pick a place you know.** A hotel with a 24-hour front desk is safer than a vague private address.
4. **Tell someone.** Share the location and time with a trusted friend.
5. **Watch for signs of coercion.** If the advertiser seems watched, rushed, or not in control of their own schedule, leave and report.
6. **Protect your privacy.** Use a WhatsApp number that is not tied to your main bank or social accounts.
7. **Respect boundaries.** Services are agreed up front; do not push for anything beyond that.
