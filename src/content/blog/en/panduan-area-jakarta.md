**South Jakarta** (Kemang, SCBD, Kuningan, Senayan, Blok M) has the highest concentration of four-star hotels and serviced apartments, so most *outcall* ads are here.

**Central Jakarta** (Thamrin, Menteng, Gatot Subroto) sits next to the business district and big malls; good for short daytime meetings.

**North Jakarta** (Kelapa Gading, PIK) is growing fast with new apartments; many outcall massage providers are based here.

Use the **area** filter on a category page to narrow your search, and the **incall/outcall** filter to choose whether you travel or the advertiser does.
