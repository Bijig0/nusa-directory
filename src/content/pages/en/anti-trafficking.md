description: "Our commitment against exploitation and human trafficking, and where to get help."
updated: 2026-09-01

We reject every form of human trafficking, exploitation and involvement of minors. Every advertiser must declare that they are an adult, post for themselves, and are not under anyone else's control.

## What we do
- A mandatory declaration before publishing, with the accepted version recorded.
- One phone number per account; numbers reused across accounts are flagged.
- An admin review queue, a report button on every ad, and account bans.
- Full cooperation with law enforcement on lawful requests.

## Need help?
If you or someone you know is being forced to work, has had documents withheld, or is being threatened:
- **Police (Indonesia)**: 110
- **SAPA 129** (Ministry of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection): 129 or WhatsApp 08111-129-129
- **Komnas Perempuan**: 021-3903963

Messages to our support email are handled with priority and confidentiality.
