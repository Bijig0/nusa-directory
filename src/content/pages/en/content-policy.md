## Allowed
- Suggestive photos (clothed, swimwear, lingerie) showing the advertiser themselves.
- Descriptions of companionship, massage and special-interest services between consenting adults.
- Rates, areas, available hours and languages spoken.

## Not allowed
- **Explicit nudity** or sexual activity in photos.
- Anyone who **appears or is implied to be under 18**, or language suggesting it.
- Signs of coercion, third-party management, or "many people available" without a clear identity.
- Phone numbers inside titles/descriptions (numbers go through the reveal system only).
- Stolen photos, celebrity photos, or photos of other people.
- Scams, requests for upfront transfers, or links to other sites.

## Moderation
Ads go live immediately and enter the admin review queue. Reported ads are prioritised. We may hide, edit or remove ads and block accounts without notice. Edited ads are reviewed again.

## Reporting
Use the **Report** button on every ad page. Reports are anonymous. For urgent concerns involving minors or trafficking, also contact your local authorities.
