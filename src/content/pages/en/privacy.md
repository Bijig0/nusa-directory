## What we collect
- **Visitors**: an anonymous device cookie (to keep your free reveal, balance and favorites), a daily-rotating IP hash for abuse prevention, and aggregate statistics (views, reveals).
- **Advertisers**: phone number/email/Google account for sign-in, ad content and photos. EXIF/GPS metadata is stripped from photos on upload.
- **Payments**: processed by Midtrans. We only store the transaction id, method and status. We never see your card number.

## Why
To run the service (sign-in, reveals, promotions), prevent fraud and abuse, meet legal obligations, and show statistics to advertisers.

## Retention
OTP codes are deleted 24 hours after expiry. Sessions end after 30 days of inactivity. Removed ads are no longer public but audit records are kept for 12 months for legal purposes. IP hashes only live for the daily rate-limit window.

## Sharing
We do not sell data. Data is shared only with the processors we need (Cloudflare for hosting, Midtrans for payments, Fonnte/Resend for code delivery) and with authorities when required by law.

## Your rights
You can change your display name, remove your ads, or request account deletion via the support email. Phone numbers reported for abuse may be blocked.

## Cookies
Functional cookies only: sign-in session, sign-in marker, anonymous device, and age confirmation. No third-party advertising cookies.
